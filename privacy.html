Last updated: July 2026

Linyi Meliex International Trade Co., Ltd. ("Meliex," "we," "us") operates the website meliexpet.cn. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

Server logs (automatically): Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may record technical data inherent to internet communication, including IP address, browser type, referring page, and time of visit. This is standard server operation and is not used by us for profiling or tracking.

No cookies, no trackers: This website does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or tracking technologies. We do not place any files on your device.

No forms, no accounts: This website does not collect personal data through registration forms, newsletter sign-ups, or user accounts.

2. How We Use Information

We do not actively process or analyze any visitor data. Server access logs maintained by our hosting provider exist solely for technical operation and security purposes (e.g., detecting abuse). We do not use this data for marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making.

3. Legal Basis (GDPR)

For visitors from the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for the minimal technical data processed by our hosting provider is legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) — ensuring the security and proper functioning of our website.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share visitor data with third parties. Our hosting provider (GitHub, Inc., USA) processes server logs as a data processor. GitHub's privacy practices are available at GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Your Rights (EEA Visitors)

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, object to processing, and data portability. Since we do not actively collect or store personal data, there is typically no personal data for us to provide or erase. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.
